Research

Questions under investigation, documented as they develop.

My current research interests sit around enterprise information systems, AI governance, and the changing role of AI in organisational decision-making.

This page documents questions I am investigating, Research Notes as those questions develop, Working Papers that may emerge from deeper study, and formal Publications. The purpose is also to preserve the research trail — including how questions, assumptions and ideas change as I read, test and learn.

From Enterprise AI Recommendations to AI Actions

How Does Delegated Discretion Change Along the Way?

September 2026 · Version 1.0

An exploratory investigation into how different forms of organisational discretion may emerge as enterprise AI moves from retrieving information and providing advice toward preparing, approving and executing organisational actions.

RN-002 · Planned

What Does Authority Mean When an AI Agent Acts on Behalf of an Employee?

RN-003 · Planned

What Makes Human Approval Meaningful Rather Than Ceremonial?

RN-004 · Planned

When an AI Agent Delegates to Another Agent, What Travels With the Task?

Will examine whether identity, purpose, restrictions, authority, context and accountability survive as work moves through multi-agent chains.
